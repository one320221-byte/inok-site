Questions

Why does INOK work when other VPNs get blocked?

We use VLESS Reality. To your provider your traffic looks like an ordinary HTTPS website visit, so it is hard to tell apart and block. If something does get blocked, we change the address and your subscription updates itself.

Which app should I pick?

If you don't want to tinker, take Incy or Happ: paste the link and connect. Happ also opens Russian sites directly, outside the VPN, so banks don't complain.

What if I lose my account number?

No problem: access is tied to your Telegram. Open the bot, tap “My access”, and the number and link are there.

How do I pay?

By card transfer or SBP. You attach the receipt and we check it by hand, usually within an hour during the day. Days add to your current term, nothing is lost.

Will INOK remove ads?

On sites and in apps, most of it: the server blocks ad networks and trackers, plus malware and phishing sites. Ads inside YouTube can't be removed this way: they come from the same servers as the video. For those you need a browser blocker or YouTube Premium.

How many devices can I connect?

Three at once on any plan: a phone, a laptop and one more.

Is there a referral bonus?

Yes. Your link is in the bot, under “Invite a friend”. When a friend pays for any plan, you get a week added.

It doesn't work. What now?

Refresh the subscription in the app and choose “Auto-select”. If that fails, send support your account number and what you see on screen: we answer fast.
